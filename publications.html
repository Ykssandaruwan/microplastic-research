Research gaps to watch

Standard methods

Sampling, contamination control, blank correction and reporting units are not yet fully harmonised across studies.

Small particles

Small microplastics and nanoplastics remain difficult to measure, especially in complex biological and environmental matrices.

Real-time monitoring

Fast field-ready tools need stronger calibration, interference testing and validation against confirmatory methods.

Human risk

More work is needed to connect exposure levels, particle properties and biological effects with confidence.

Transformation history

Environmental ageing can change detection signatures and biological behaviour.

Open datasets

Machine learning workflows need transparent reference datasets that represent real-world particle diversity.
